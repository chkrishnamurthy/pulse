Every training screen planned for Pulse

35 screens, drawn in the real colours of your app. These are pictures only: nothing is built yet. All numbers are examples. Look, choose, and I will plan only what you keep.

1. Look

Go from A to H. Every screen has a number and a short reason.

2. Choose

Press Keep, Change or Remove. Write a note if you want something different.

3. Tell me

Say “done” in our chat. I can read your choices and notes from this page.
